# Sirius Black

## [CANON]

- Gryffindor student of the Marauders generation.
- One of the four Marauders.
- Animagus form: large black dog.
- Co-creator of the Marauder's Map.
- Born into the Black family but rejects much of its ideology and expectations.

## [GAME] Design direction

Systemic identity may emphasize:
- high practical magical confidence;
- risk-taking;
- tracking / scent in Animagus form;
- social consequences of the Black family name;
- tension between school identity and family expectations.

## Open questions

- how family pressure enters school simulation;
- when and how his break with family becomes visible;
- distinction between Sirius and James in combat and exploration.
