# Peter Pettigrew

## [CANON]

- Gryffindor student of the Marauders generation.
- One of the four Marauders.
- Animagus form: rat.
- Co-creator of the Marauder's Map.

## Design principle

Peter cannot be treated as «the useless fourth character» simply because later canon frames him as weaker than James and Sirius.

The game needs a reason the other three genuinely spent years with him.

## [GAME] Potential systemic identity

Rat form can make Peter uniquely valuable for:
- infiltration;
- tiny spaces;
- eavesdropping;
- bypassing physical barriers;
- reconnaissance;
- discovering routes nobody else can physically access.

This gives him gameplay value without rewriting later character history.

## [GAME] Trust and future betrayal

Peter must not read as an obvious future traitor from the beginning.

The later betrayal only carries weight if earlier trust was reasonable.

During school years he should be capable of:
- keeping important secrets;
- taking real risks for the group;
- performing useful reconnaissance;
- being the right person to trust in some situations;
- earning genuine inclusion through repeated shared experience.

Foreshadowing, where used, should remain interpretable in more than one way at the time it occurs.

Do not encode later canon as an always-on hidden `traitor` personality flag that contaminates every school-year decision.

## Open questions

- what Peter is genuinely good at as a student;
- how insecurity develops over the years;
- how to foreshadow later choices without making betrayal feel predetermined from Year 1.
