# Peter Pettigrew

## [CANON]

- Gryffindor student of the Marauders generation.
- One of the four Marauders.
- Animagus form: rat.
- Co-creator of the Marauder's Map.

## Design principle

Peter cannot be treated as «the useless fourth character» simply because later canon frames him as weaker than James and Sirius.

The game needs a reason the other three genuinely spent years with him.

## [GAME] Potential systemic identity

Rat form can make Peter uniquely valuable for:
- infiltration;
- tiny spaces;
- eavesdropping;
- bypassing physical barriers;
- reconnaissance;
- discovering routes nobody else can physically access.

This gives him gameplay value without rewriting later character history.

## Open questions

- what Peter is genuinely good at as a student;
- how insecurity develops over the years;
- how to foreshadow later choices without making betrayal feel predetermined from Year 1.
