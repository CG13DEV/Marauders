# Lily Evans

## Status

Character design seed. Canon profile should be expanded separately from source-backed material.

## [GAME] Design principle

Lily is a complete world entity, not:
- a moral compass for James;
- a romance quest giver;
- a reward for choosing correct dialogue options;
- a passive participant waiting for the player.

She must have independent goals, friendships, academic interests, conflicts, beliefs, memories and relationships that continue without James present.

## [GAME] Relationship with James

No `RomanceMeter` or guaranteed romantic trajectory.

Her opinion of James should emerge from:
- observed behavior;
- shared events;
- information received from other people;
- accumulated salient memories;
- changes in James's actual behavior over time.

A playthrough in which Lily respects James but still finds him irritating, distrusts him, remains distant, or never becomes romantically interested is a valid world state unless a later project decision explicitly constrains the chronology.

## [GAME] Character integrity

Lily should be intelligent and principled without becoming an oracle.

She may:
- misunderstand people;
- have blind spots;
- make emotionally costly decisions;
- hold a wrong belief for good reasons;
- refuse the player even when cooperation would be mechanically convenient.

The target feeling is not «the writer decided what Lily must do for this scene», but:

> given who she is, what she knows and what she remembers, of course she did that.

## Open questions

- which events can legitimately change her opinion of another character.
