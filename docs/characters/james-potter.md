# James Potter

## Status

Character design seed. Expand only with source-backed canon or explicitly marked game interpretation.

## [CANON]

- Gryffindor student of the Marauders generation.
- One of the four Marauders.
- Animagus form: stag.
- Owner/user of the Invisibility Cloak during school years.
- Co-creator of the Marauder's Map.

## [GAME] Design direction

James should not reduce to «fighter».

Potential systemic identity:
- confident practical magician;
- high mobility;
- leadership inside the group;
- strong physical traversal in Animagus form;
- important connection between exploration and the Invisibility Cloak.

## Open questions

- individual magical strengths by school year;
- relationship progression with Lily;
- rivalry with Snape without flattening either side;
- how leadership appears in co-op without making James the mandatory protagonist.
