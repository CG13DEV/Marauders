# Remus Lupin

## [CANON]

- Gryffindor student of the Marauders generation.
- One of the four Marauders.
- Werewolf.
- His condition motivates James, Sirius and Peter to become Animagi.
- Co-creator of the Marauder's Map.

## [GAME] Design direction

Remus must not be designed as «the one without an Animagus form».

Potential strengths:
- theory and research;
- advanced understanding of magical rules;
- investigation;
- preparation;
- access to knowledge;
- periodic werewolf state as a major systemic event.

## Werewolf cycle

The lunar cycle should affect:
- planning;
- secrecy;
- friendships;
- access to locations;
- risk;
- NPC suspicion.

## Open questions

- exact simulation of transformations;
- player agency during werewolf state;
- how much control is appropriate;
- what information professors know at each stage.
